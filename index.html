GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find clear, honest answers about our raw honey, sourcing practices, and WhatsApp ordering.

Unlike ultra-filtered commercial brands that pasteurize honey at high heat and remove beneficial pollen, Madhuram Honey is 100% raw, unheated, and naturally strained. This preserves all the active digestive enzymes, antioxidants, and authentic floral flavors.
We partner directly with ethical beekeepers across lush wildflower foraging zones in Tamil Nadu. The bees forage freely on diverse native blossoms without chemical exposure, ensuring natural multifloral variety.
Madhuram Honey is currently available in our flagship 750 ml glass bottle priced at ₹650 (inclusive of taxes).
Simply select your quantity on the product card and tap "Order on WhatsApp". A pre-formatted message will open in WhatsApp. Once you send it, our team will confirm your delivery address and share dispatch details.
We deliver nationwide across India. Every glass bottle is packed using multi-layer drop-tested protective packaging to ensure safe, spill-free doorstep delivery.
Crystallization is the ultimate natural proof of raw, unpasteurized honey rich in natural glucose and pollen. If your honey crystallizes during cooler weather, simply place the glass bottle in a bowl of warm water (under 40°C) to reliquefy gently.
